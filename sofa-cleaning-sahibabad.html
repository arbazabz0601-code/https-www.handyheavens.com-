Four warning signs for your sofa

Small mistakes at home can make a stain harder to remove. Keep these in mind before we arrive.

Do not rub

Rubbing pushes a spill deeper into the fabric. Blot gently instead.

Do not soak

Too much water can leave rings and a damp smell. Less is more.

No harsh chemicals

Bleach and strong cleaners can fade colour and weaken fabric.

Do not wait

Fresh spills lift far more easily than old ones. Call us early.
