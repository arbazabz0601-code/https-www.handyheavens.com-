Know your fabric before you clean it

Every sofa material needs a different touch. Pick yours to see how we clean it and what to avoid at home.

Fabric sofas

Cotton and polyester-blend sofas collect dust, sweat and food marks in the seats and arms.

What we do

  • Vacuum loose dust and crumbs first
  • Pre-treat marks and spills
  • Deep clean with controlled moisture

Please avoid at home

  • Scrubbing hard with a wet cloth
  • Soaking the cushions
Get a quote for my fabric sofa

Velvet sofas

Velvet looks rich but shows pressure marks and spills quickly.

What we do

  • Clean gently along the direction of the pile
  • Use low moisture so the nap stays soft
  • Brush the pile back once dry

Please avoid at home

  • Hot water and stiff brushes
  • Rubbing in circles, which leaves patches
Get a quote for my velvet sofa

Leatherette sofas

Leatherette wipes clean, but dirt settles into seams and the surface can dry out.

What we do

  • Wipe with a mild, surface-safe cleaner
  • Clean seams and folds where dirt collects
  • Finish with a protective wipe-down

Please avoid at home

  • Alcohol wipes and strong chemicals
  • Sharp scrapers on dried marks
Get a quote for my leatherette sofa

Jute and textured weaves

Textured and jute-blend weaves trap dust deep between the fibres.

What we do

  • Dry vacuum deep into the weave
  • Use light moisture and careful extraction
  • Dry quickly so the weave keeps its shape

Please avoid at home

  • Soaking, which can darken the fibre
  • Hard brushing that frays the weave
